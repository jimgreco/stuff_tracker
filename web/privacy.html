Privacy

CubbyLog Privacy Policy

Last updated July 7, 2026

What CubbyLog Stores

CubbyLog stores the inventory information you choose to enter, including homes, locations, item names, notes, quantities, item properties, serial numbers, model numbers, purchase dates, warranty dates, estimated values, photos, and documents.

Account And Sync

You can start using CubbyLog locally on your device. If you sign in with Apple or Google, CubbyLog stores account identifiers such as your email address, name, provider ID, and session information so it can sync your inventory, restore your data, and support shared homes.

Photos, Documents, And Sharing

If you add hosted photos or documents, those files are uploaded so they can sync across devices and be available to collaborators you invite to a home. Shared homes are visible to invited collaborators according to their access level.

Subscriptions

CubbyLog uses App Store purchase information to apply subscription access. The backend stores subscription product IDs, transaction identifiers, status, and expiration or revocation details needed to keep your account plan accurate.

What CubbyLog Does Not Do

CubbyLog does not sell personal information. CubbyLog does not use third-party advertising tracking. CubbyLog does not use your inventory content to build advertising profiles.

Support

If you email support, CubbyLog uses the information you provide to respond to your request. Contact support@cubbylog.com with privacy questions or account support requests.
